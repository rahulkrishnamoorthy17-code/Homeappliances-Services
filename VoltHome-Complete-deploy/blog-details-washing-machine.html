7 signs your washing machine needs service

Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Washing machines usually give warning signs before a breakdown becomes serious.

Unusual noise or vibration

New grinding, banging or excessive movement can point to an unbalanced load, worn bearing or installation issue.

Water is not draining normally

Standing water, slow drainage or recurring drain errors should be checked before the pump or hose is damaged further.

The cycle takes much longer

Repeatedly extended cycles can indicate sensor, heating, inlet or load-detection problems.
